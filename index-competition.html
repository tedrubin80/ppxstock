Plain English Summary

What the numbers mean

10,000 simulations of Adobe's re-rating path, drawn from 5 historical precedents with matching fundamental mechanics, show a median 24-month price of $478 — nearly double today's $242 entry. The expected value across all simulations is $431 (+78%). Even under a bear scenario (P25), the stock reaches $265 — a modest 10% gain. The simulation assigns only a 15.1% probability to a negative return at 24 months. The asymmetry is clear: the downside is bounded by franchise value, the upside is bounded only by how fast the market reprices earnings quality.

Methodology

10,000 iterations. Returns sampled from a normal distribution fitted to 5 comparable re-rating events (μ=107.6%, σ=21.1% at 24 months). Failure rate 15% (CEO uncertainty). Bear tail 12% (guidance cut risk). DCF ceiling applied at bull case. Not financial advice.
