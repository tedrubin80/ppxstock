What Breaks the Thesis
Honest risk disclosure

CEO succession goes poorly
An external hire with a fundamentally different strategy (e.g., cost-cutting vs. AI investment) would delay the re-rating and potentially impair long-term growth.
2
Firefly ARR growth decelerates materially
If Firefly ARR stalls below $500M or AI-influenced ARR share fails to reach 40%, the "AI tailwind" narrative weakens and the re-rating timeline extends.
3
Enterprise NRR drops below 100%
Net revenue retention falling below 100% would signal actual customer attrition — validating the bear case that AI tools are replacing rather than augmenting Adobe's platform.
Methodology & Sources

This thesis uses a P/E-based scenario analysis applied to consensus FY2027 EPS estimates ($28.25). Three scenarios are probability-weighted: Bear (25% at 15x P/E), Base (50% at 22x P/E), Bull (25% at 30x P/E). The "market-implied disruption probability" is derived from the discount of ADBE's current P/E (14.03x) to the peer median (35.85x).
